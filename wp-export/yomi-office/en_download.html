🧭 Which package should I choose?

Quick guide:
  • Most Android phones (2016 and newer): Pick the arm64-v8a column matching your Android version (Android 10+ covers most modern phones).
  • Older / budget 32-bit Android phones: Pick armeabi-v7a. Android emulators and Intel Chromebooks use x86_64.
  • Windows 10 / 11 PCs: Pick Win10-11-x64 Setup (.exe) for quick setup, .msi for enterprise deployment, or portable.zip to run without installing.
  • Windows 7 SP1 / 8.1 PCs: Pick the Win7-8.1 builds (x64 or x86), or the universal AnyCPU package.
