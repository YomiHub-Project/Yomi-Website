Core Features

Engineered for extreme performance, minimal footprint, and premium user experience

📜

Detailed Natal Charts (Bửu Đình Method)

Places all 14 major stars (with brightness) and 70+ minor stars, the Four Transformations, Tuần/Triệt, the Long Life – Doctor – Grand Duke rings, 10-year and yearly periods, annual stars and the Life/Body masters following the Bửu Đình method. Export PNG, QR code, print and keep many profiles.

🌙

Astronomical Lunar Calendar 1900–2099

Vietnamese lunar calendar (GMT+7) using Hồ Ngọc Đức's algorithm, checked on all 73,018 days from 1900 to 2099 (no invalid day, lunar↔solar conversion round-trips). Stems and branches, Nạp Âm, 24 solar terms, auspicious days and hours, 12 Trực, holidays.

📚

Bửu Đình Master Library (611 Articles)

611 original articles in 13 chapters of the Bửu Đình books: major and minor stars, palaces, the 72 patterns, star placement, periods, sample charts, a dictionary… Search with or without Vietnamese accents on the device, bookmarks, continue reading, adjustable text size.

🔍

Astrological Research Assistant

Ask about a palace, star or period, or pick Life palace, Career, Wealth, Marriage, Health or This year. The assistant analyses the current chart and quotes the related Bửu Đình articles – fully offline, not generative AI.

💎

Liquid Glass Engine (With On/Off Switch)

Liquid-glass refraction on the title bar, tab bar and dialogs, frosted glass on cards. Image work runs on a background thread with caches for smoother scrolling. On/Off switch and a lighter frosted mode for low-end devices.

🌍

68 Languages & Auto-Update Check

Localized into 68 international languages. The built-in checker looks for new versions on GitHub Releases (can be turned off), downloads and installs over the old one, keeping your data.
